# Image suggestions for the 2027 Water Quality sheet

The sheet is words only (5 pt, 0 margins, two sides). The 2027 rules allow the sheet "in any form and from any source", so if you give up some text you can paste small pictures. Each one below is worth roughly 1 inch x 1 inch. Listed from highest to lowest payoff.

## Part I and III (diagrams)
1. **Estuary circulation cross-sections**: salt wedge, partially mixed, well mixed, fjord with sill (and inverse). Tested on almost every invitational.
2. **Estuary geology block diagrams**: coastal plain, bar-built, fjord, tectonic (plus delta shapes).
3. **Wastewater plant flow**: screen/grit, primary clarifier, aeration tank, secondary clarifier, disinfection, with the RAS/WAS loop. Potable-water train next to it (coagulation, flocculation, sedimentation, filtration, disinfection).
4. **Nitrogen cycle** (N2, NH3/NH4+, NO2-, NO3-) and **phosphorus cycle** (no atmosphere).
5. **Carbonate chemistry**: CO2 + H2O to H2CO3 to HCO3- to CO3 2-, speciation vs pH graph, and the NOAA aragonite saturation map (2000 vs 2050; blue = high, red = low).
6. **DO vs temperature solubility chart** and the **DO sag curve**; BOD bottle diagram.
7. **Survivorship curves** (Type I/II/III), **logistic curve with K**, and an **age-structure pyramid** trio (expanding, stable, declining).
8. **Energy pyramid (10% rule)** and an **inverted biomass pyramid**.
9. **Water cycle** (labeled), **T/S/density vs depth profiles** (thermocline, halocline, pycnocline), **ocean zones** block diagram.
10. **Five garbage patches** gyre map; **Ekman transport / upwelling** sketch; **spring vs neap tide** diagram.
11. **Salinometer**: hydrometer sketch (ballast, stem) and a blank calibration graph (position vs % NaCl).

## Part II (identification photos, one tiny thumbnail each)
12. Banded coral shrimp, crown-of-thorns starfish, triton, pencil urchin (thick blunt spines), long-spined black sea urchin (thin needles), butterflyfish (eye-band + false eyespot), parrotfish, bumphead parrotfish (vertical forehead), humphead wrasse (hump + thick lips), barramundi cod, Nassau grouper (tuning-fork forehead stripe), snapper, sweetlips (and juvenile), moray eel, sea cucumber, giant clam, demosponge, Gorgonia sea fan, flamingo tongue snail (live mantle vs bare shell), hard coral, fleshy algae (red / green / brown).
13. Coral polyp cross-section (tentacle, mouth, gastrovascular cavity, mesenteries, skeleton); coral life cycle (spawn, planula, settle, polyp, colony); reef types fringing -> barrier -> atoll; reef zones (flat, crest, slope).
14. NOAA Degree Heating Weeks / bleaching alert table (Watch, Warning, Alert 1-5).
