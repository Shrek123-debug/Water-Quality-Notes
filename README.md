# Water-Quality-Notes

- `tests/` - past Science Olympiad Water Quality tests and answer keys, plus the 2027 sample tournament.
- `resources/` - 2027 rules, training handouts, Castro & Huber *Marine Biology*, Tait *Elements of Marine Ecology*, Tomczak *Intro to Physical Oceanography*, Reef Check indicator-organism report, and other study material.
- `notes/` - the two-sided 4 pt cheatsheet (`Water_Quality_Cheatsheet.docx`, plus a plain-text copy) and `Image_Suggestions.md`.

Cheatsheet settings: Letter, 0 margins, Arial 4 pt, single line spacing, no paragraph spacing. Upload the .docx to Google Docs (or paste the .txt) and keep those settings so the text fills exactly the front and back.
